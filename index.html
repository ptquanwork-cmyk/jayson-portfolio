Stay inthe work

I am Pham The Quan — most people call me Jayson. Finance student at UEH, and someone who kept taking on the work nobody had assigned yet.

That is how the profile ended up looking like this. Valuation models and GARCH studies on one side. Eight months of venture programme operations at BLOCK71 Vietnam on the other. A signed brewery client where I wrote the contract, built the Odoo system and delivered the financial model alone. Agent workspaces and a research vault I run every day. The thread through all of it is the same: I take something scattered and hand back something a team can act on.

What I am actually good at is the join between analysis and execution. I can build the model, and I can also build the sheet the client fills in without breaking it, the dashboard their manager reads on Monday, and the runsheet forty volunteers work from. Plenty of people can do one of those. The value is in doing both, because the model that nobody can use is not finished.

Where I am heading: business and data analysis, venture and programme operations, and AI-built internal tools. Finance is the training. Making things work in practice is the point.
