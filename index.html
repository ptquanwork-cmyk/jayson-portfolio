My strength is not only analyzing problems, but also organizing them into practical systems, dashboards, plans, and outputs that teams can actually use.

I am currently shaping my direction as a Business and Data Analyst, venture/program operator, and AI workspace builder, someone who connects finance, data, strategy, execution, and AI-enabled productivity to create practical value in fast-moving business environments.
